About us

Renovation without surprises

SIA “CONSTRUCTA” is a Riga construction company entered in the Latvian register of construction companies. We carry out apartment and house renovation, interior finishing and specialist construction work.

CallWrite to us

Who we are

A small company, direct communication

We are a small company, and that is our advantage: you talk to the person who is personally responsible for the work. We start every project with a site visit and an estimate, and we agree every change with you before the work is carried out.

In Riga we work on apartments, private houses and older buildings, where care and patience are often needed.

A good renovation is one you do not have to think about a year later.

Principles

How we work

  1. 01

    Estimate before work starts

    Before we start, you know the list of works, the price and the timeline.

  2. 02

    One contact person

    From the first call to the handover you deal with one person.

  3. 03

    Changes only after agreement

    If something changes during the work, we first discuss it with you.

  4. 04

    A tidy site

    After the work we leave the rooms clean and ready to use.

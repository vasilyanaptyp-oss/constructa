Services

Apartment and house renovation

From a single room to a whole apartment or house. We plan the work in sequence, so you know what is happening at every stage.

CallWrite to us

A renovation starts with a site visit. Together we walk through the rooms, note what needs to change, and prepare an estimate with a list of works and materials. That way it is clear before we start what will be done and what it will cost.

We can take on the whole renovation or only part of the work, for example walls and ceilings before the furniture is installed. If you live in the apartment during the renovation, we agree the order of work in advance.

What is included

  • Strip-out and room preparation
  • Levelling of walls and ceilings
  • Filling and painting
  • Plasterboard partitions and ceilings
  • Floor levelling and floor coverings
  • Bathroom and toilet tiling
  • Door installation and finishing of openings
  • Cleaning after the work

Questions

Frequently asked

Can you renovate just one room?

Yes. We work on a single room as well as on a whole apartment or house. We define the scope at the site visit.

How long does an apartment renovation take?

It depends on the floor area and the scope of work. We write the specific timeline into the estimate before work starts.

How does the price change if something new turns up during the work?

We first show it to you and discuss it with you. We start any additional work only after your approval.

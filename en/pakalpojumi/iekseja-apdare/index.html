Interior finishing

Smooth walls and straight corners are what you notice every day. We work with plasterboard, plaster, filler and paint.

CallWrite to us

We build plasterboard partitions, suspended ceilings and niches, plaster and fill walls, and prepare them for painting or wallpaper.

For painting we choose a suitable primer and paint, so that the surface looks even under raking light as well.

  • Priming

Questions

Frequently asked

Is a plasterboard partition strong enough?

Yes, if it is built correctly. Where shelves or a TV are planned, we add extra reinforcement to the frame.

How many coats of paint do walls need?

Usually two coats after priming. Dark shades, or a light colour over a dark surface, may need more.

Can you work in an occupied apartment?

Yes. We cover the furniture and floors, plan the dusty work in advance and tidy the work area every day.

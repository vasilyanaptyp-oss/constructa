Reconstruction

New construction

We construct new buildings as a general contractor: we manage the work from start to handover and keep you informed at every stage.

Price

How the price is set

  1. 01

    Site visit

    We name the price after visiting the site, not over the phone, because every property is different.

  2. 02

    Estimate with a list of works

    The estimate shows all the work, the materials and the timeline, so you can decide without pressure.

  3. 03

    Changes only after agreement

    If something new comes up during the work, we first discuss it with you and only then continue.

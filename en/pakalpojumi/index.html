Services

Renovation and finishing in Riga

Six areas of work that we handle every day. Each has its own page listing the work involved and answering the most common questions.

01

Apartment and house renovation

From a single room to a whole apartment or house. We plan the work in sequence, so you know what is happening at every stage.

Includes

  • Strip-out and room preparation
  • Levelling of walls and ceilings
  • Filling and painting
  • Plasterboard partitions and ceilings
Learn more
02

Kitchens

We prepare the kitchen so that the units and appliances fit without surprises: straight walls, exact measurements and careful tiling.

Includes

  • Removal of the old finishes
  • Wall levelling for the kitchen units
  • Tiles above the worktop
  • Floor levelling and covering
Learn more
03

Bathrooms

In a bathroom, what matters most is what stays under the tiles. That is why we give waterproofing and the substrate as much attention as the final finish.

Includes

  • Removal of old finishes and tiles
  • Levelling of walls and floor
  • Waterproofing of wet areas
  • Wall and floor tiling
Learn more
04

Floors

A good floor starts with a level base. Before laying the covering we check the surface and, if needed, level it.

Includes

  • Removal of the old covering
  • Substrate check and levelling
  • Self-levelling compound and underlay
  • Parquet laying
Learn more
05

Interior finishing

Smooth walls and straight corners are what you notice every day. We work with plasterboard, plaster, filler and paint.

Includes

  • Plasterboard partitions
  • Suspended ceilings and niches
  • Wall plastering
  • Filling and sanding
Learn more
06

Façades and specialist work

Façade renovation and other work that requires a registered construction company. Tell us about the building and we will assess how best to approach it.

Includes

  • Façade survey
  • Restoration of damaged plaster
  • Repair of cracks and joints
  • Priming and painting
Learn more

Price

How the price is set

  1. 01

    Site visit

    We name the price after visiting the site, not over the phone, because every apartment is different.

  2. 02

    Estimate with a list of works

    The estimate shows all the work, the materials and the timeline, so you can decide without pressure.

  3. 03

    Changes only after agreement

    If something new comes up during the work, we first discuss it with you and only then continue.
